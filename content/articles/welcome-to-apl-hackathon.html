If you have never been to a hackathon, the word can sound intense. Ours is not. The APL Hackathon is a Monday meetup at the Attleboro Public Library where neighbors sit together, work on projects, ask questions, and share what they learned.

Who it is for

Absolute beginners, students, career switchers, hobbyists, and professionals who want a calm place to build. You do not need a finished idea. You do not need to know how to code. Showing up is enough.

How a typical day goes

  1. Arrive, grab a seat, and introduce yourself in the opening circle.
  2. Optionally pick a workshop idea — or keep working on your own project.
  3. Build, browse tutorials, or pair with someone nearby.
  4. Share a quick demo or reflection at the end if you like.

What helps

  • A charged laptop (library computers are available if you need one).
  • Headphones for focus or tutorial videos.
  • A notebook for sketches and sticky notes.
