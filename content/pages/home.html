About

Come as you are

The APL Hackathon is a low-pressure monthly meetup at the Attleboro Public Library for anyone curious about making things with code, design, data, or hardware. Beginners are welcome. So are seasoned builders who enjoy helping others get unstuck.

We meet in the library’s 2nd floor tech lab, optionally pick a workshop idea, and spend the session working on personal or group projects. There are no prizes and no pressure to finish — just a friendly place to learn in public.
